Consent Form

This study is about the usability evaluation of the G6 Market system, a mobile online shopping app prototype developed to improve the online shopping experience. You are invited to participate because your feedback is very important in checking if the system is user-friendly and easy to use. The goal of this study is to evaluate how well the prototype works and find ways to improve it based on the user experience, specifically measuring its ease of use, design and appearance, navigation, and overall user experience.
If you agree to participate, you will be asked to (1) briefly review and sign this consent form, (2) interact with the G6 Market mobile app prototype by exploring its features, (3) complete the System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire based on your experience, and (4) optionally answer open-ended feedback questions. The whole process will take about 10 to 15 minutes only.
Your participation in this study is voluntary. This means you are free to participate or not, and you can also stop at any time without any problem. All the information you provided will be kept private and confidential. Your identity will not be shown or shared with others, and your answers will only be used for academic purposes and system improvement. There will be no risk in participating in our survey except for the small amount of time you will spend. Your feedback will help us to improve the G6 Market system and make it more useful for future users.
